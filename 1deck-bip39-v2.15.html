1-Deck Casino Card → BIP-39 Seed Generator

Physical draws only. This version contains no random number generator of any kind. Every entropy bit comes from cards you shuffled, drew and typed in by hand. Nothing else is an input.

1 · Set up your physical protocol

Wash the deck first. Spread all 52 cards face-down on the table and swirl them around with both hands for at least 30 seconds, then square them up. A wash destroys the factory order far faster than riffling does, and every entropy figure below assumes the order is gone. Only then riffle, seven times or more, with a cut or a strip between each.

Use a sealed deck bought new from a shop (Bee, Bicycle, Copag 310…). Remove jokers and advertising cards — 52 cards exactly. Do this on an air-gapped machine.

3 · Result

The entropy is the leading bits of SHA-256 over your exact card sequence, so all 128 (or 256) entropy bits come from cards on the table. The BIP-39 checksum is then computed from that entropy exactly as the standard defines — checksum bits are redundancy, not entropy, so there is nothing to draw for and nothing to choose.

6 · Self-test (fully deterministic)

    

        There is no random number generator here to measure, so these tests prove the mapping is correct
        instead: the official BIP-39 vectors, SHA-256 known-answer tests, golden vectors that pin the entire
        card-to-seed pipeline to fixed expected output, and a sweep of 8192 derivations checked against an
        independent BIP-39 implementation and round-tripped back to entropy. Same result on every machine, every
        time. A golden vector fails only if this page has been altered.
        The third button runs the heavy test: it substitutes every card position with every legal
        alternative and demands the seed change each time. That is deliberately not a chi-square over
        many generated seeds — SHA-256 whitens its output, so a build that threw away a quarter of
        your cards would still pass every distribution statistic. It does not survive this one.

How it works

            

                An ordered 4-card draw carries 22.63 bits. Every draw you enter is pooled into one SHA-256
                extraction, so a 12-word seed needs 8 draws and a 24-word seed 14. All 128 or 256 entropy bits come
                from your cards; the BIP-39 checksum is computed from them, as the standard defines.
            

        

        

            
Physical protocol

            

                • Sealed 52-card deck, jokers removed.

                • Wash first: cards face-down on the table, swirled for 30 seconds or more.

                • Then riffle seven times or more, with a cut or strip between each.

                • Draw 4 cards off the top, in order, and write them down.

                • Order matters. Left-to-right as dealt.
            

        

        

            
Security

            

                


                Save it and run it offline. Use an air-gapped machine.
                Never type a real seed phrase on an internet-connected device.
